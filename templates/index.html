Getting Started in 4 Steps

Follow these simple steps to create your account, register your pet, and book your first appointment.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email and create a secure password. You'll receive a verification code.

  • Enter full name, email, phone & address
  • Set a strong password (8+ chars)
  • Check email for the 6-digit code
2

Login to Dashboard

Use your verified email and password to sign in and access your dashboard.

  • Enter your registered email & password
  • First-time users see a welcome celebration
  • Forgot password? Use "Forgot Password?"
3

Register Your Pet

Add your pet's details — name, breed, age, gender, weight, allergies, medical history.

  • Go to My Pets → "Add New Pet"
  • Upload a photo, fill in details & allergies
  • Age can be entered in Years or Months
4

Book Appointments

Schedule grooming, vaccination, or check-ups with real-time slot availability.

  • Go to My Appointments
  • Select pet, services, date & time
  • Pay via GCash or Maya to confirm
Step 2 of 3

Complete System Guide

Everything you can do inside the PetLink system — explained step-by-step.

Account Setup & Login

  1. Register: Click "Register" → fill out the form → submit
  2. Verify Email: Enter the 6-digit code sent to your inbox
  3. Login: Use your email & password → click "Sign In"
  4. Forgot Password: Click "Forgot Password?" → click the reset link
  5. Profile Settings: Update name, phone, address & profile picture

Pet Management

  1. Add Pet: "My Pets" → "Add New Pet" → upload photo → save
  2. Edit Pet: Click "Edit" on any pet card to update info
  3. Change Photo: Click the camera icon on the pet avatar
  4. Delete Pet: Click "Delete" → confirm (cannot be undone)
  5. Allergies: Select from a list or add custom allergies

Booking Appointments

  1. Open Booking: "My Appointments" → "Book New Appointment"
  2. Choose Pet: Select which pet the appointment is for
  3. Select Services: Pick one or more services
  4. Pick Date & Time: Available dates shown on calendar
  5. Confirm & Pay: Review → pay via GCash or Maya
  6. Cancellation: Cancel unpaid appointments anytime
  7. Retrieve: Cancelled appointments can be retrieved

Smart Pet Care (AI)

  1. Access: Go to "Smart Pet Care" on your dashboard
  2. Generate: Click "Get Recommendations"
  3. Read: See personalized tips for vaccination & health
  4. Take Action: Click "Book Appointment" from a recommendation
  5. Medical Records: View your pet's full medical history

Messages & Communication

  1. Open Messages: Click "Messages" on the sidebar
  2. Choose Recipient: Chat with Admin, Staff, or Veterinarian
  3. Send Message: Type your message and press "Send"
  4. Emoji & Images: Use 😊 for emojis, 🖼️ to upload photos
  5. Real-Time Chat: Replies appear instantly

Payments & Receipts

  1. Pay Now: Click "Pay Now" to pay via GCash or Maya
  2. Pay Later: Skip and pay anytime from "My Appointments"
  3. Secure Gateway: Payments processed securely by Xendit
  4. Receipt: Click "View Receipt" to print or save
  5. Payment Status: Track unpaid / paid / failed
